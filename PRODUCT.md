# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Technical recruiters, engineering hiring managers, founders, and peer developers evaluating Alex Chen for senior frontend and full-stack engineering roles or high-impact consulting engagements.

## Product Purpose
Showcase production-grade full-stack engineering capabilities, architectural depth, and measurable delivered impact through an immersive, high-craft personal portfolio website. Success means memorable first impression, effortless navigation, and direct inbound contact for engineering opportunities.

## Positioning
An engineering portfolio anchored by an ambient cinemagraph hero and backed by detailed, quantified case studies (Lighthouse scores, scale metrics, architecture choices) rather than generic résumé bullet points or boilerplate templates.

## Operating Context
Viewed primarily on modern desktop and mobile browsers by busy hiring teams reviewing dozens of candidate portfolios. The site must be instant to load, responsive across all viewports (375px to 4K), and completely free of layout thrash.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Fast single-page application built with React, Vite, and TypeScript.
- Full-bleed background video hero with seamless cinemagraph loop and user playback controls.
- Interactive project showcase with categorized filtering, live demo links, and repository access.
- Structured technical skills matrix with verified proficiency and experience metrics.
- Detailed career timeline with concrete performance and business impact metrics.
- Direct contact mechanism with one-click email copy and interactive inquiry form.

## Product Principles
1. **Craft Over Cliché**: Reject generic AI portfolio tropes (no nested cards, no purple gradients, no decorative gradient text).
2. **Speed & Smoothness**: Zero layout thrash, GPU-accelerated transforms, instant interactions.
3. **Artifact-First**: Let the work, architecture, and live projects speak with verifiable data and clear evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA minimum across all text and UI elements.
- Semantic HTML landmarks (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<footer>`, `<nav>`, `<article>`).
- Full keyboard navigability with visible focus indicators.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` across all animations and video playback.
